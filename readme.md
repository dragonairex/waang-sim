# 橫閃(橫)
A 2000's "007入子彈"-style in-person multi-player battle game designed by a group of primary school kids

## Rules
- 2+ players
- Rock-paper-scissors-style turn-based battle, everyone chooses their move, reveals their chosen move for the turn at the same time, and resolves real-time
- Free-for-all or 2-team battle
- Become the last man standing to win the game

### Setup
- Everyone comes together to form one big circle
- Everyone holds their hands up in front of their chests.
- Each player holds their right palm facing down, on top of the left palm of the player on your right; similarly, left palm facing up, below the right palm of the player on the left
- If there are only two players left, they face each other and holds their palms on/under the other player's opposite palm
- Everyone starts at 0 energy[氣]
- Everyone starts at 1 life
- Everyone starts at Ground level (level 0)

### Playing
- Everyone syncs their rhythm. This game is a rhythmic battle
- Everyone starts their turn by saying "橫" and at the same time touches the palms of the player(s) next to you
- Then, each player picks one move, says the name of the move, and does the posture of that move, to use the move successfully
    - Saying the name of non-attacking moves can be omitted, especially when the battle becomes intense. However, saying out load names of attacks is a must
    - If an attack was used, the attacking posture must point to the other player he or she wishes to attack
    - The name of the move and the posture done by the player must match. Otherwise, the move fails
    - All attacks can only target one other player
    - An attack costs energy. If the player did not have the necessary energy to pay the attack cost, the move fails
- After every move, the game is resolved to see if one or more player faints (see [Resolving Attacks])
    - If any player was attacked by a successfully attacking player, in range of the attack, and did not successfully present a protective or counter move, then they faint. You only have 1 life
    - Fainted players leave the game. They move out of the circle
    - The two players next to the fainted player who are still alive moves closer to reform the correct palm up/palm down posture to create a smaller circle
- After all attacks of the round are resolved, the next round begins with the remaining players
- Game continues until only one player (free-for-all) remains, or all the remaining players are from the same team (2-team battle)

## Field
- The field consists of ground level([地面], level 0), 999 upper levels called Heaven[天堂], and 999 lower levels called Hell[地獄]. Hell[地獄] levels are labeled with negative numbers -1 to -999

## Resolving Attacks
- If a player was attacked by another player, their status and moves are resolved to see if their defense stands
### A. Successful use of an attack
- An attack is treated as successful if the attack user:
  1. has enough energy to use the attack of his or her choice,
  2. announces the correct name of the attack (or its alias), and
  3. performs the correct posture of the chosen attack, pointing at a target (any remaining other players)
- Once the attack is deemed successful, the energy cost of that attack is deducted from the attack user's held energy
### B. Whether the target player is in attack range
- The current location of the attacking user and the target player are used to calculate relative location (distance)
- If the distance between the attack user and the target player is smaller than or equal to the range of the used attack, the target player is in attack range
- If the target player is out of range, the attack misses
### C. Move used by the target player
- Whether the target player gets hit by an attack is determined by the move he or she chooses
- If the target player uses a non-attack move, the attack(s) are resolved based on whether the move can protect against, reflect, absorb the attack, or moves out of range
- If the target player uses an attack move, but the attack is direct at a third player (not the attacking player), then the target player is effectively no-guard (must still be in range to be hit)
- If the target player also uses an attack directed at the attack user, the attacks are resolved to determine the outcome
  - If both attacks have the same energy cost, they cancel out. Both attacks still count has successfully used (energy is still deducted)
  - If one of the attacks have higher energy cost than the opposing attack, it wins and the player with the lower energy cost attack is hit (faints)

## Advanced Rules
- In the case of 2-team battle where one team has a man-down, a selected player of the one-man-down team can have 2 lives before starting the game so both teams have the same total number of lives
