# 橫(橫閃)
橫(橫閃) is an in-person multi-player battle game from the 2000's, "007入子彈"-style, designed by a group of primary school kids.

## Rules
- 2+ players.
- Rock-paper-scissors flow. Players choose a move, show the move at the same time, and resolve actions.
- The goal is to hit other players with attacks and avoid hits to stay alive.
- Free-for-all or 2-team battle.
- Become the last man standing to win the game.

### Setup
- Form 1 big circle with all players.
- Hold both hands out in front of your chest.
- Put your right palm down on top of the left palm of the player on your right. Put your left palm up below the right palm of the player on your left.
- If only 2 players remain, face each other. Put your palms on top of or below the other player's opposite palm.
- Every player starts at 0 energy[氣].
- Every player starts at 1 life.
- Every player starts at Ground([地面], 0) level.

### Playing
Everyone syncs their rhythm. This game is a rhythmic battle.
1. Start each turn together:
   - Say "橫".
   - Touch the palms of the adjacent player(s) at the same time.
2. Pick 1 move for the turn:
   - Say the move name and do the move posture to use the move correctly.
   - For a non-attack move, you may omit saying the name, especially during fast play. 
   - For an attack move, you must say the name of an attack move out loud.
   - For an attack move, point the attack posture at 1 target player. An attack cannot target more than 1 player.
   - Move name and the posture must match. If they do not match, the move fails.
   - Verify you have enough energy to pay the attack cost. If you do not have enough energy, the move fails.
3. Successfully using an attack on a turn means you successfully released an attack, regardless of if you get hit or even faint on the same turn. 
4. Resolve hits and faints:
   - A player faints if a successful attack hits them, is in range, and is not stopped by a valid defense or counter.
   - A fainted player leaves the game and moves out of the circle.
   - Surviving players next to fainted player(s) move closer to make a smaller circle and reform the palm-up and palm-down positions.
5. Start the next round with the remaining players after all attacks resolve.
6. Continue rounds until 1 player remains in free-for-all, or only 1 team remains in a 2-team battle.


## Playing Field
- The playing field consists of levels: ground([地面], level 0), upper levels called Heaven[天堂] (1 to 999 and higher), and lower levels called Hell[地獄] (-1 to -999 and lower).
- Count ground level [地面] (0) as 1 level when you calculate distance.

- Posture: Place both fists flat near your body at each side of your waist with palms facing up.

Name: 保
- Effect:
  - Protect yourself from all Ha[波] and Fist[拳] attacks that cost 6 energy or less. 
  - Attacks that cost 7 energy or more bypass 保 and hit you.
- Posture: Cross both arms in front of your chest with straight fingers. 

Name: 上
- Effect:
  - Move up 1 level.
  - If you were in Hell[地獄], move directly to level 1 of Heaven[天堂].
- Posture: Hold 1 hand with 1 finger pointing up.

Name: 落
- Effect:
  - Move down 1 level.
  - If you were in Heaven[天堂], move directly to level -1 of Hell[地獄]. 
- Posture: Hold 1 hand with 1 finger pointing down. 

Name: 反
- Effect:
  - Reflect Ha[波] attacks that cost 7 energy or less back to the attacker. 
  - [反] cannot reflect Fist[拳] attacks.
  - You cannot reflect attacks that cost 8 energy or more.
  - If attacks of both Ha[波] and Fist[拳] types target you, only Ha[波] attacks reflect and Fist[拳] attacks hit you. 
  - Attacker(s) faint when reflection succeeds.
- Posture: Raise both hands in front of you with straight fingers pointing up and palms facing toward you.

Name: 反拳

  - Attacker(s) faint when reflection succeeds.

Name: 吸

  - [吸] cannot absorb Fist[拳] attacks.

## Resolve Attacks
- If a player was attacked by another player, their status and moves are resolved to see if their defense stands
### A. Successful use of an attack
- An attack succeeds if the attacker meets all 3 conditions:
  1. Has enough held energy to pay the attack cost.
  2. Says the correct attack name or alias.
  3. Uses the correct attack posture and points at 1 remaining player.
- Deduct the attack energy cost as soon as the attack succeeds.
### B. Check attack range
- Compare the attacker's current level and the target player's current level. 
- If a player used [上] or [落] on this turn, use that new level. 
- If the target player is in range, the attack can hit. 
- If the target player is out of range, the attack misses.
### C. Check move used by the target player
- If the target player uses a non-attack move, resolve the attack against protection, reflection, absorption, or out-of-range movement.
- If the target player uses an attack against a 3rd player (not the attacker), the target player has no guard and can be hit if in range.
- If the target player also attacks the attacker, the two attacks collide, regardless of range:
  - If both attacks have the same energy cost, both cancel out. Both attacks count as successful and both players pay their energy costs.
  - If 1 attack has a higher energy cost, that attack wins and the player with the lower-cost attack is hit (and faints).

## Advanced Rules
- In a 2-team battle where 1 team has 1 fewer player, before the game begins, 1 selected player on the smaller team can start with 2 lives (can withstand 1 attack) so both teams have the same total lives.
- In a 2-team battle, friendly fire is on by default.
